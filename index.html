The problem

Plans are easy to present. Keeping sight of them is harder.

Product work often spans annual goals, quarterly outcomes, initiatives and delivery teams. When the work moves forward, a roadmap alone does not show which decisions are waiting or which dates need a conversation.

TrueKanban gives you a product-level view of that work. It surfaces recorded blockers and date signals, then lets you open the underlying initiative to understand the context.

FAQ

Questions worth asking.

Do I need to move my delivery team into TrueKanban?

No. A product board can stand on its own. You can add delivery-team boards under it when that is useful. Direct Jira synchronisation is not available yet.

What makes an initiative need attention?

The summary reflects information recorded on the initiative, such as a blocker, a passed or approaching target, or a missing target. These are signals for a conversation, not automated judgments about your team.

Will I get a forecast on day one?

Sample initiatives can demonstrate the forecast, but their numbers are illustrative. A live comparison needs enough completed initiatives with dates on your board. Until then, TrueKanban says that the history is insufficient.

Is the pilot a paid subscription?

We have not published a subscription price or final plan limits. Request access and we will explain the current pilot arrangement before you decide whether to participate.
