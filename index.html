Before you try it

What to expect from the pilot.

How Candour fits your tools, your team and your decisions.

Does it replace Jira or Linear?

Candour gives product managers a shared view of goals, initiatives, attention signals and delivery evidence. You can keep detailed issue tracking in the tools your teams already use. Candour's signals are based on the data recorded on its product board.

Can I import work or connect other systems?

Candour can import cards from CSV files into delivery-team boards. Future iterations may connect with other systems, including agent-triggered work. A product board can also stand on its own.

What can I share with stakeholders?

Invite colleagues to a board so they can review its summary, initiatives and delivery comparison together. Automated stakeholder reports and exports are planned for a future iteration.

Who can see my boards?

Board access is managed by invitation. A product-board invitation can also include selected delivery-team boards, without granting access to every board. Read our privacy information for how we handle personal data, or ask us a question before putting sensitive work into the pilot.

What makes an initiative need attention?

The MVP is designed to bring blockers and target dates together with early signals from initiative ageing and the stability of comparable delivery work. These signals should prompt a conversation, not make an automated judgment about a team. The age check in the journey above illustrates that direction.

Will I get a forecast on day one?

Sample initiatives can demonstrate the forecast, but their numbers are illustrative. A live comparison needs enough completed initiatives with dates on your board. Until then, Candour says that the history is insufficient.

How reliable is a historical comparison?

Past delivery is a useful signal when there is enough comparable work and the delivery system is reasonably stable. Both sample size and variation matter: a wide spread of completion times makes any date less certain. Candour's product signals should make that context visible, so forecasts support a discussion rather than promise an outcome.

What does the pilot cost?

The invited pilot is free, with a review after 90 days and no automatic charge. We are considering Free, £29/month Practitioner and £149/month Team plans. Pilot participants who choose a paid plan will receive 50% off for their first 12 months, with final terms explained before they decide.
